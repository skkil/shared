# Content style guide: <product>

_Mechanics only; voice lives in `voice.md`, terms in `glossary.md`. Defaults follow Google's and Microsoft's style guides for English and 국립국어원 rules for Korean; change any line and date the change._

## Capitalization

- Headings and titles: sentence case. Buttons and labels: sentence case ("Save changes").
- Product and feature names: as in the glossary.

## Numbers, dates and times

- English: numerals for 10 and above and all measurements; "October 6, 2026" in prose, `2026-10-06` in tables; 24-hour time in technical docs.
- Korean: 2026년 10월 6일 in prose, 2026.10.06. or 10/6(화) in reports; 24-hour time in formal docs; 1,200원; counters after numerals (3개).

## Punctuation

- English: serial comma; closed em dashes, rarely; no end punctuation in headings, buttons or labels.
- Korean: a period ends ~합니다/~해요 sentences, including UI sentences; no trailing colon; fewer commas than English.

## Lists and tables

- Numbered lists for sequences, bulleted lists otherwise; parallel grammar within a list.
- Tables for comparisons; a header row; no merged cells.

## Formatting

- `Code font` for code, commands, file names, keys and values. **Bold** for UI element names in instructions (Korean software strings use [ ] when the product follows Microsoft's convention).
- No italics in Korean text.
- Link text names the destination.

## Korean spacing choices

- Auxiliary verbs: spaced (도와 드리다) | attached (도와드리다). Choose one.
- Proper nouns: spaced by word | by unit. Choose one.
